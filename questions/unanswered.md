# Unanswered

*Questions the worked example never had to answer. A gap in a built system is usually a question that business answers off the top of its head, differently every time, and those are the ones a second shop trips on.*

**Status: open, 2026-09-14. Nothing here writes a field yet, which is why none of it is in the bank proper.**

## Found by their absence

These came out of reading a working system and noticing what was not in it. Each one is asked on real calls and currently has no written answer.

1. **What payment methods are accepted at the door?** Cash, card, check. The agent is asked this and has nothing to say.
2. **Is a deposit ever taken for a large-parts job?** A job with an expensive part ordered in advance is exposure if the customer walks away. No policy exists.
3. **Is a fee charged on a no-show or a no-access visit?** The drive happened either way. This decides whether it was free.
4. **How long is a repair guaranteed?** Parts pass through a manufacturer warranty. The labor has no stated term at all.
5. **What happens on a repeat failure outside the free-recall window?** Nothing between "free" and "full price" is written down.
6. **Is there any same-day override for a genuine emergency?** Same-day is a hard no with no exception below the life-safety interrupt. Water coming through a ceiling will test it.
7. **Is pricing ever tiered by appliance, brand or complexity?** The fee is flat whether it is a dryer or a built-in wall oven. Flat is a choice and it has not been examined.
8. **How long do parts usually take, and is the customer told without asking?** A customer waiting on an ordered part has no expectation set and no update coming.
9. **How does a wrong booking get caught after the fact?** Every rule in the system is a prevention rule on the way in. Nothing checks a booked job for a wrong address, a wrong brand, or a caller who was qualified incorrectly.
10. **Are there referral incentives, loyalty pricing, or a tipping policy?** Nothing exists customer-side.
11. **What changes when there is more than one technician?** This is the biggest one. The calendar, the dispatch, the arrival windows and the phone agent are all built around exactly one person, and nothing anticipates the transition. A second technician is not a setting.
12. **What happens when multi-supplier routing meets a second live supplier?** The logic to choose between suppliers exists in code and has only ever run with one supplier connected. It is untested rather than proven.

## Not found, and not looked for

These are not evidence from the worked example. They are questions this bank does not ask and a real business has to answer, listed so the gap is visible rather than implied.

13. **What language does the business work in, and what happens to a caller who does not speak it?** The bank is monolingual throughout and never asks.
14. **Who answers the phone when the agent is down completely?** The fallback chain covers a failed booking, not a failed system.
15. **How long is customer data kept, and what happens when somebody asks for it to be deleted?** Retention is not asked anywhere in the setup, and it is a legal question in a growing number of places. It gets the independent second check.
16. **What does the customer get told about the agent being an agent, in writing, after the call?** Question 4 covers the live call. Nothing covers the confirmation.
17. **Who is allowed to change an answer in this bank once the business is running?** Chapter 11 leaves the re-ask path open. This is the other half of it: not when an answer goes stale, but who has the authority to replace it.

## How an entry leaves this file

It leaves when somebody can say what it writes and what changes if it is answered the other way. Until then it stays here, because a question with an interesting discussion and no wired effect is the thing chapter 11 exists to keep out of the bank.
